About

I am a Lecturer in CSE at BUET, where I teach operating systems and database systems, and an M.Sc. student advised by Sukarna Barua. I received my B.Sc. in CSE from BUET in 2023, graduating 2nd of 129 with a CGPA of 3.98/4.00.

My recent research looks at where energy goes when large models run on AI accelerators. Modeling TPU-class NPUs showed me that compute dominates energy, but memory (SRAM and HBM) takes a larger share than expected, and that hardware-side savings depend on how the model itself computes. I now want to work on efficient machine learning across the stack, from model architecture down to the hardware.

I am applying to Ph.D. programs in Computer Science and Computer Engineering for Fall 2027.

Research projects

AstraWattch: energy modeling for TPU-class accelerators

Mar 2026 – Sep 2026
With Prof. Tanvir Ahmed Khan (Columbia University), remote
  • Modeled the energy use of ML workloads on TPU-class NPUs by combining ASTRA-sim, STAGE (compute requirements of each workload) and ReGate (NPU power gating, MICRO 2025).
  • Found that compute dominates energy use, but on-chip SRAM and off-chip HBM account for a surprisingly large share.
  • Showed that ReGate misses power-gating opportunities in Mixture-of-Experts inference. Capturing them needed only small changes to ReGate, so we judged it too incremental to pursue.
  • On hold: TPUs expose no public power-measurement interface, so the models could not be validated against real hardware.

Isotropy of pretrained protein language models

Terraces in species tree inference (B.Sc. thesis)

BUET, advised by Prof. Md. Shamsuzzoha Bayzid
